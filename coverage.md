## Deliberately not computed from these workbooks

- AD acquisition request counts, decoded network-byte accounting, archive history, GEO twelve-library map, preparation independence, study treatment doses/timing and reported publication counts. These are source/protocol/provenance statements, not discoveries from the selected workbook. Row-total-minus-overlap arithmetic is computed, but does not prove the original plotting procedure.
- PD caption panel mapping, source protocol/pooling/read threshold/DESeq version, source reported selection totals, historical label correction and original figure-input universe. Caption constants are transcribed distinctly; GS/GSDA discrepancy remains unresolved. Source-table selection membership is verified, not original Figure 2 plot identity.
- PDAC Table 3 / endpoint-map Figure 2, exposure durations/doses/n, AHA percentages, viability/colony outcomes, synergy models/p-values, 20213 mapping statement and all original biological/model interpretations. These require source text, not the selected worksheet. PDAC original Figure 1F identity and full tested universe are unknown.
- Original DESeq/RiboDiff fits, corrections and p-values, raw sequencing, independent animals/patients, confidence intervals, inferential tests, enrichment, therapeutic efficacy and cross-disease validation. These are neither reconstructed nor silently approximated.
- Manuscript/DOCX formatting, rendered figure pixel identity, acquisition ledgers, AI/human contribution history, declarations, licenses and journal eligibility. All need their own evidence/review.

Reference construction used existing verified numerical JSON/CSV outputs, not extension-run outputs. Sparse collision/boundary records are supplied for auditability; full memberships are stored only as hashes. Actual runtime comparison, source hash checks and artificial software-test outcomes are recorded by build_test.py outside the ZIP to avoid circular artifact hashes. Identity checks constrain parsing/selection under shared semantics, not scientific interpretation or original authors' endorsement.
